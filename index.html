      Numbers: Wikimedia pageviews, fetched live from Wikimedia's own API: the top 1,000 English Wikipedia articles for one day in UTC, across desktop, mobile web and the app. Each day is published some hours after it ends in UTC; until it is, you get the day before. Left out: the Main Page and pages that are not articles; pages whose traffic is nearly all desktop or nearly all phone, which Wikimedia's own Top 25 Report treats as automated; disambiguation pages and pages without a short description; and anything whose title or description is about sex, porn, killing, suicide, terror attacks or disasters, because this is a game. Add ?day=2026-10-26 to the address to play any day since 1 July 2015. Everyone gets the same ten. Nothing leaves your browser except the requests to Wikipedia.
