Chapter 1 · The one that works

Three in five

Chapter 2 · Where it lives

It is the films

This split is a look after the fact: unlike the six hints, it was not tested against random labels of its own.

Epilogue · What to tap

New beats famous

Fame doesn’t show on the card: being a person, being American, being alive tells you nothing about yesterday. A seventh hint I tried, the younger of two living people, won ( of ), and is not drawn. What does show is that a thing is new.

So in Which Read More?, when one of the two is a film from this year or last, tap it. It will still be wrong two times in five.
